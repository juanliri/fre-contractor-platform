Our Story

From the Bronx to Every Borough

F.R.E. Contractor LLC was founded in the Bronx with a simple mission: deliver the kind of high-quality painting service that every homeowner deserves — not just the ones who can afford the big, impersonal crews.

We built our reputation one room at a time. Word spread through neighborhoods, apartment buildings, and contractor networks. Today we serve residential clients across all 5 boroughs and have completed commercial projects for national brands including T-Mobile and Chuck E. Cheese.

Everything we do is still personal. You'll talk to the owner. You'll meet the crew. And you'll see the difference that real craftsmanship makes.
