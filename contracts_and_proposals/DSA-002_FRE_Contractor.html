This Agreement establishes the legal framework for the deployment of Client's digital orchestration infrastructure. TodoBuild ("Agency") is authorized to engineer an **Odoo-Enterprise** environment as defined in the **O-6 Full Online ERP** specification on Page 2.

2. Recurring SaaS Pass-Through

Client acknowledges that the Odoo Platform and Cloud Hosting are third-party SaaS services. Agency will facilitate the initial setup; however, upon delivery (Milestone 3), Client is responsible for all recurring subscription, licensing, and hosting fees billed directly by the platform provider (Odoo S.A. or equivalent). TodoBuild LLC d/b/a TodoBuild assumes no responsibility for these third-party costs.

3. Digital Handoff & Liability Release

Upon the successful deployment of the O-6 environment to a live production state, Agency's primary development obligations are fulfilled. Agency is not responsible for platform uptime, data loss, third-party security breaches, or system failures originating from the Odoo infrastructure or Client's subsequent administrative modifications. Technical support post-handoff is governed by the separate Managed Service Registry on Page 3.

3. Liability & Advisory Shield (v12 ORCH-001)

IMPORTANT: Client acknowledges that TodoBuild LLC d/b/a TodoBuild is a management and consulting firm. Orchestrator does not perform physical construction, home improvement work, or physical installations. All physical labor, demolition, and construction will be performed by independent, licensed execution partners ("Subcontractors"). Agency's total liability is capped at the total amount paid by Client.

4. Software & IP (v12 TECH-002 / ND-008)

The software interfaces, 3D models (Titan OS), and backend dashboards are licensed on a subscription basis and remain the exclusive intellectual property of TodoBuild Technologies Inc. Unless a buyout is executed, Agency retains full ownership of all architectural renderings and system schemas.
