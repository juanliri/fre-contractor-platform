What Our Interior Package Includes

We never skip steps. Every surface receives rigorous multi-stage preparation before the first coat of paint ever touches your wall.

1. Surface Preparation & Repair

We spackle all nail holes, patch drywall cracks, tape compromised seams, and sand walls to a flat Level 4 or Level 5 smooth surface.

  • ✓Caulking all baseboards, door frames, and crown molding
  • ✓Priming water stains, smoke damage & patched areas

2. Premium 2-Coat Application

Heavy canvas drop cloths cover hardwood and tiles, while static plastic film seals all furniture, electronics, and lighting fixtures from airborne dust.
